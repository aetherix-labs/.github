### What We Engineer
Aetherix Labs is an independent hardware, industrial automation, and local AI engineering lab based in Malaysia. We bridge physical electronics and deterministic control systems with on-premise, privacy-first artificial intelligence.

* **Embedded Electronics & PCB Prototyping:** Custom circuit design, multi-layer PCB layout, and real-time microcontroller firmware (`STM32`, `ESP32`, `C/C++`, `Rust`).
* **Industrial Automation & Test Systems:** Automated end-of-line (EOL) hardware testing, PLC/SCADA tooling, and deterministic visual control software.
* **Sovereign Local AI Infrastructure:** On-premise LLM deployment, model fine-tuning, and private multi-agent engineering workflows without cloud lock-in.
